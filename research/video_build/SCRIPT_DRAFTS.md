## VIDEO 1: Ronaldinho at 46 (target 60-70 s, British English voice-over, one narrator)
Structure = hook, then context, then argument, each beat opens with a hook line.

1. HOOK: A 46-year-old just made his debut in Italy's third division. His name is Ronaldinho.
2. WHO: In case you are too young: World Cup winner. Champions League winner. Ballon d'Or. He is the only player in history to win the World Cup, Copa América, Champions League, Copa Libertadores and the Ballon d'Or.
3. YOUTH HOOK: But the weird part started when he was a kid. At eight they called him Ronaldinho, "little Ronaldo", because he was the smallest boy on the pitch. At thirteen he scored all twenty-three goals in a twenty-three-nil win. All of them.
4. PEAK HOOK: Then came the World Cup. 2002, Brazil against England. A free-kick from forty yards. It curled past David Seaman into the top corner. Seven minutes later he was sent off. That is peak Ronaldinho: genius, then a red card.
5. BARCELONA: At Barcelona he won the 2005 Ballon d'Or. Real Madrid's own fans stood up and applauded him at the Bernabéu. The first Barça player to get that since Maradona.
6. FALL HOOK: So what happened? Football moved on. Last club before Ravenna: Fluminense, 2015, nine games. Officially retired in 2018.
7. WHY RAVENNA HOOK: So why is a 46-year-old legend playing for Ravenna? Ravenna are in Serie C, Italy's third division. Their owner, Ignazio Cipriani, announced the signing at the World Cup in June. Ronaldinho is a player and a minority shareholder, and some reports said his role would mostly be marketing. He says he wants a 300th career goal.
8. DEBUT HOOK: And on Sunday, against Forlì, he walked out in white. Flares. Smoke. He took a corner in the nineteenth minute and the keeper saved it. Ravenna won two-nil.
9. CLOSER: Forty-six years old, one corner kick, and still the most famous man in Serie C. Your uncle from Sunday league has no more excuses.

## VIDEO 2: Mudryk and the Neymar video message (target 70 s, interview audio in Ukrainian with English captions + one British narrator)
1. HOOK: In 2022 Neymar recorded a personal video message for a 21-year-old Ukrainian footballer. This was his reaction.
2. WHO: Mykhailo Mudryk. Shakhtar Donetsk winger, famous for pace. That autumn he scored against Leipzig and Celtic in the Champions League and was named Ukrainian Footballer of the Year.
3. SETUP HOOK: The interviewer asks: which top club would you love to play against? [INTERVIEW CLIP, English captions] Mudryk: Barcelona. Camp Nou was the childhood dream.
4. TEASE: The interviewer: I'm surprised you didn't say PSG, because there is someone there who doesn't seem to mind meeting you. [Neymar video plays: "Hi, Misha Mudryk. I hope you have many good moments in your career. Bye-bye."]
5. REACTION: Asked if he'd like to face Neymar, Mudryk says yes: he grew up watching Neymar's videos until he was 18 or 19, and he was his favourite footballer. [INTERVIEW CLIP, English captions]
6. PAYOFF HOOK: So how did those "many good moments" go? Two months later Mudryk joined Chelsea: seventy million euros up front, the most expensive Ukrainian footballer ever.
7. THE LONG PART (serious, gentle): Then a long wait. He was provisionally suspended in December 2024 over a doping test; in July this year all charges were dropped. Chelsea said that under today's testing standards the sample would not have been reported as positive. He came back in August after twenty months without a competitive match.
8. NOW HOOK: And now? September: a season-long loan to Tottenham, reunited with Roberto De Zerbi, the manager who helped him break through at Shakhtar.
9. CLOSER: Neymar wished him "many good moments". Fair play: that was a lot of moments.
